# Studio Owner's Guide

A short, non-technical guide to running your website. No coding needed — everything is done from the **admin
panel** in your browser.

> _This guide is finalised with real screenshots during Phase 13. The steps below reflect the intended flow._

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to `https://your-site.com/admin`.
2. Enter the email and password we gave you.
3. First time in, you'll be asked to set a new password. Please do — it replaces the temporary one.

## 2. Adding a project

1. In the sidebar, click **Projects → New project**.
2. Fill in the title, a short summary, and the main description.
3. Choose a category, location, and year.
4. Click **Save**. The project is a draft until you publish it.

## 3. Uploading photos

1. Open the project and go to the **Media** tab.
2. Drag photos in (or tap **Upload** on your phone).
3. Every photo needs a short **alt text** describing it (for accessibility and Google). Tick _decorative_ if it
   carries no meaning.
4. Drag photos to reorder them. Click a photo's star to make it the **cover**.

## 4. Publishing

Click **Publish**. Your project appears on the public site within a few seconds. To hide it again, set it back
to **Draft** or **Archived**.

## 5. Adding a team member

**Team → New member** → name, role, short bio, photo, and social links → **Save**.

## 6. Approving an editor's work

If you've invited an editor and turned on **verification**, their changes wait for you:

1. Go to **Pending approvals**.
2. Review the change (you'll see a before/after).
3. Click **Approve** to publish, or **Request changes** with a note back to them.

## 7. Changing the look

Go to **Appearance**:

- **Accent colour** — pick from 24, or set your own hex (it warns if text would be hard to read).
- **Background pattern** — pick from 22, and slide the opacity.
- **Motion** — choose how much animation the site uses (Off / Subtle / Refined / Expressive).
- Use **Preview** to see changes on your real homepage before you **Save**.

## Need help?

Contact us at the support email on your handover sheet. For anything technical (domains, backups, hosting),
see [`DEPLOYMENT.md`](./DEPLOYMENT.md).
